What We Do

Zulu Logistics is a dedicated freight solutions provider specializing in truck dispatch services for independent owner-operators and small fleets across the United States. We are committed to helping trucking professionals maximize their revenue by streamlining operations, securing high-paying loads, and offering flexible carrier partnerships.

With a deep understanding of the logistics industry, Zulu Logistics serves as a reliable backend support system — managing everything from load booking and broker communication to rate negotiation, paperwork, and compliance. Our dispatching team works around the clock to ensure that drivers stay on the road, earning more with less hassle.
