AI LOAD BOOKING

How to Use AI Load Booking

Smarter loads, more revenue and less hassle — here is how our AI-powered booking works for you, step by step.

HOW IT WORKS

5 Simple Steps

You don't need to learn any new app. Just share your truck details once — our AI and dispatch team do the rest.

  1. 01

    Tell Us About Your Truck

    Fill out the quote form with your truck type, trailer, MC number, preferred lanes and home time. This is what the AI matches loads against.

  2. 02

    AI Finds Matching Loads

    Our AI tools scan available loads and rank the ones that fit your equipment, lanes and schedule, so good options surface in minutes, not hours.

  3. 03

    Your Dispatcher Reviews & Negotiates

    A real dispatcher checks every match, talks to the broker and negotiates the rate in your favor. AI speeds things up; people make the call.

  4. 04

    You Approve & Drive

    You get the load details and rate confirmation, say yes, and roll. We handle the paperwork and broker follow-ups.

  5. 05

    Your Next Load Is Already Lined Up

    While you are on the road, the AI looks ahead for your next load near your drop-off, to cut empty miles and downtime.

WHY IT MATTERS

AI Speed. Human Judgment.

Faster Booking

Matches surface in minutes, so you spend less time waiting for a load.

Less Downtime

Next loads are planned ahead, which helps reduce empty miles and sitting days.

Better Rate Decisions

Load and lane data helps your dispatcher push for stronger rates.

Real People, 24/7

AI never replaces your dispatcher. A real person is always there to talk to.
